1. **Academic Decision-Support Simulation Only:**

2. **No Classified or Real Operational Data:**  
   The application does **NOT** contain, transmit, or process classified defence information, encrypted tactical datalinks, real-world bridge structural certificates, or operational armed forces deployments.
3. **Synthetic Node Catalogs:**  
   All military station coordinates, delay multipliers, and axle tolerance limits are synthetic or derived from publicly available reference material for simulation modeling only. Under no circumstances should real-world operational security (OPSEC) data be entered into this demonstration platform.
