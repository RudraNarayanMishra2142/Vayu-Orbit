## 1. PROJECT IDENTIFICATION

VAYU ORBIT is an academic research prototype developed by **Rudra Narayan Mishra** within the School of Computer Science & Engineering at Lovely Professional University. The project addresses Problem Statement PDS-3 through a unified web-based decision support system incorporating:
- Multi-criteria topological corridor routing.
- The deterministic VORAM (Vayu Orbit Risk Assessment Model) risk heuristic.
- Vehicle payload and axle load manifest enforcement.
- Road Opening Party (ROP) recon workflows.
- Authoritative role-based access control.
